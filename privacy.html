Webfile helps you keep track of Companies House filing deadlines and reminds you before they’re due. To do that, we need a small amount of information about you and the companies you add. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We’ve tried to keep it plain and short.

1. Who we are

Webfile is operated by [Company legal name], a company registered in England and Wales (company number [number]), with its registered office at [address]. For the purposes of UK data protection law, we are the “controller” of the personal data described in this policy.

Webfile is an independent service. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Companies House or GOV.UK.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password (stored securely as an encrypted hash, never in plain text).
  • Companies you add: company names and numbers, and the filing dates we fetch for them from the public Companies House register. This information is already public.
  • Reminder settings: when and how you’d like to be reminded, and any extra recipients you add, such as your accountant’s email address.
  • Messages you send us: anything you include when you contact us or fill in a form on this website.
  • Basic technical information: such as your browser type, device and the pages you visit, used to keep the service secure and working properly.
  • Payment details (paid plans only): handled by our payment provider, [payment provider]. We don’t see or store your full card details.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • create and run your account;
  • track the filing deadlines of the companies you add and send you reminders;
  • stop reminders once Companies House shows a filing as done;
  • reply to your questions and give you support;
  • keep the service secure and fix problems;
  • tell you about important changes to the service or these policies.

Our legal bases for this are: performing our contract with you (providing the service you signed up for), our legitimate interests (keeping the service secure and improving it), and legal obligations (for example, keeping financial records). If we ever want to send you marketing emails, we’ll ask for your consent first, and you can withdraw it at any time.

We don’t sell your personal information, and we don’t use it for advertising.

4. Who we share it with

We only share your information with trusted service providers who help us run Webfile, and only as much as they need. These include:

  • website and app hosting: [hosting provider];
  • email delivery for reminders: [email provider];
  • payments for paid plans: [payment provider].

We also look up company information from the public Companies House register using its official data service. We may share information if the law requires us to.

Where a provider stores data outside the UK, we make sure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as the UK’s International Data Transfer Agreement or an adequacy decision.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account information for as long as your account is open. If you close your account, we delete or anonymise your personal information within [90 days], except where we need to keep certain records for longer by law (for example, invoices for six years).

6. Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that’s wrong;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • object to, or ask us to limit, how we use it;
  • ask us to transfer your information to another service;
  • withdraw consent where we rely on it.

To use any of these rights, email us at [privacy@webfile.com]. We’ll reply within one month. If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk, although we’d appreciate the chance to put things right first.

7. Cookies and storage

This website doesn’t use advertising or tracking cookies. It stores one small preference in your browser: whether you chose light or dark mode. It also loads fonts from Google Fonts and an animation library from cdnjs, which means those services receive your IP address when the page loads.

If we add analytics in future, we’ll update this policy and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

8. Keeping your information safe

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and limited access for our team. No system is completely secure, but we work hard to protect your data and will tell you promptly if something goes wrong that affects you.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make important changes, we’ll let you know by email or with a notice in the app. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.

10. Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy or your information, email [privacy@webfile.com] or write to us at [registered address].
