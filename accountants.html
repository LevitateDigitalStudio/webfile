Questions

Good to know

Can we import all our clients at once?

Yes. Paste a list of company numbers or upload a CSV exported from your practice software, and Webfile picks up each company’s deadlines from the Companies House register.

Does Webfile replace our practice software?

No. It focuses on Companies House deadlines and works alongside the tools you already use, through import, export and calendar feeds.

Will our clients get emails from Webfile?

Only if you switch client reminders on. You choose which clients receive them and when.

Who in the team can see what?

You can assign clients to team members so each person sees their own list, while partners and managers see the whole portfolio.

Does Webfile file for us?

No. Your team files through your usual route. Webfile makes sure the deadline stays in view until it’s done.

Is Webfile part of Companies House?

No. Webfile is independent and isn’t affiliated with Companies House or GOV.UK.
