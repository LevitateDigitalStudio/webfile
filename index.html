Questions

Good to know

Does Webfile file for me?

No. You or your accountant file as usual. Webfile just reminds you.

Doesn’t Companies House already send reminders?

Yes, and they’re worth having. Webfile adds one view of all your companies, with reminders that continue until the filing is done.

When are accounts and confirmation statements due?

Accounts are usually due 9 months after your financial year ends. A confirmation statement is due at least once every 12 months. Webfile shows the exact dates for each company.

Who is Webfile for?

Company directors, people running several companies, and accountants keeping an eye on client deadlines.

How much does it cost?

It’s free for up to 3 companies. If you look after more, see the plans above.

Is Webfile part of Companies House?

No. Webfile is independent and isn’t affiliated with Companies House or GOV.UK.

Will Webfile cover other deadlines?

That’s the plan. HMRC deadlines, insurance renewals, contracts and licences are on the way.
